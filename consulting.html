What I do

  1. Statistical analysis and reporting. From descriptive tables to mixed models, survival analysis, and Bayesian methods.
  2. Machine learning and AI for clinical data. Imaging, records, and text: model development, validation, and honest reporting.
  3. Study design and protocols. Sample size, endpoints, pre-registration, and analysis plans.
  4. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses. PRISMA-compliant, with risk-of-bias assessment and reproducible code.
  5. Scientific writing and figures. Manuscripts, grant sections, and publication-quality figures.
  6. Teaching and workshops. Biostatistics and machine learning for clinical teams, in Spanish or English.

How an engagement works

  1. A free 30-minute discovery call to understand the problem and the data.
  2. A written quote: scope, deliverables, timeline, and price. Hourly from US$ 120 (S/ 250 in Peru) or a fixed price per project.
  3. Work in short cycles with a shared document and code you keep. Invoice by PayPal on delivery.
